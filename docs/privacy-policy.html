UniMate

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

UniMate is a student app for campus links, socials, and news. This policy explains what we collect and why. It is the privacy policy URL for the UniMate app on the App Store.

Who we are

UniMate is an independent student app. It is not an official product of your university. For privacy questions, email unimate.app@proton.me.

Information we collect

  • Device identifier. UniMate stores a device id on this install so we can create and recognise your student profile.
  • Study profile. University, program, year of study, and language that you choose in the app.
  • Role. Student, moderator, or admin, as set on the UniMate server for your account id.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a push token so we can send news alerts to this device.
  • Local links. Extra Home links you add yourself stay on this device (local storage). They are not uploaded as part of your server profile.
  • News images. Moderators and admins may attach a photo when publishing news. That image is stored so other students can see the post.

We do not read your mailbox, campus timetable, or Microsoft account.

How we use it

  • Show Home buttons, social links, and news for your university and program.
  • Remember your language and notification preferences.
  • Let moderators and admins publish content for the audiences they manage.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party sites

Home buttons and social links can open university websites, Canvas, email, or other services. Those sites have their own privacy policies. UniMate is not responsible for what they collect.

Retention and your choices

You can change university, year, and language in Menu. To ask us to delete server-side profile data tied to your account id, email unimate.app@proton.me and include the id shown at the bottom of Menu.

Children

UniMate is made for higher-education students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
