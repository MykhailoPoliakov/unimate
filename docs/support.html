What UniMate is

UniMate helps students find campus services, social links, and news for their university and program. It is an independent student app, not an official university product.

Course content, timetables, and mail stay on university or Microsoft systems. If a campus site is down, contact that service directly.

Common help

  • Wrong university or year: open Menu and update Studies, then save.
  • Notifications: enable them in Menu if you want news alerts.
  • Privacy and data: see the privacy policy.
